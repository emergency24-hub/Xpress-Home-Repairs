XPRESS HOME REPAIRS LTD — LOCAL WEBSITE

OPEN THE WEBSITE
1. Extract the whole ZIP (do not open index.html inside the ZIP).
2. Open xpress-home-repairs/index.html in a current browser.
All pages, images, styles and scripts are local. No install, server or build step is needed.
JavaScript must be enabled. Telephone links use your device's calling application.

EDIT CONTENT
Open script.js in a text editor. The SITE block at the top contains the business
details, phone, optional email and domain, services, testimonials and FAQ answers.
Save the file and refresh the browser. Keep quotation marks and commas intact.
An email address automatically adds a mailto link to each page's contact section.
For domain, enter the full https:// address once known.
The HTML page titles/descriptions are in the five .html files. Colours and layout
are in styles.css. To change photos, replace the matching files in assets/.

INCLUDED
index.html — home, services, about, customer words, areas, FAQs and contact
plumbing.html — plumbing services
heating.html — gas, oil, LPG and heating systems
electrical.html — electrical services
emergency.html — emergency callouts
script.js, styles.css, assets/ — shared content, behaviour, styling and images

CONTENT NOTES
Business information, statistics and testimonials use the supplied material.
No numerical Google rating was provided, so none is displayed.
Email: info@xpresshomerepairs.uk. Domain: https://xpresshomerepairs.uk.
Only Hampshire and surrounding areas are stated as coverage; customers are asked
to call with their postcode. No opening hours or guaranteed arrival times are added.
Fee, quote and boiler coverage answers direct customers to confirm by phone.
Accreditation logos use the supplied artwork in its original colours; no registration
numbers have been invented. Keep these logos current with your business status.
No enquiry form is included: this offline site contacts the business by telephone
and, when configured, your visitor's email application. It does not collect submissions.

HOSTING
Upload these files and the assets folder together to a static web host.
No analytics, external fonts, cookies, third-party scripts or network calls are used.
The site includes descriptive page titles, metadata and local-business structured data.

VALIDATION
Desktop and mobile browser checks covered navigation, service pages, image loading,
FAQ expansion and phone link targets. Local file references and ZIP contents were
checked. The configured email link targets info@xpresshomerepairs.uk.
